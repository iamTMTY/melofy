# Chrome Web Store screenshot backgrounds

The background photographs are by Pawel Czerwinski and are free to use under
the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The screenshot cards use
the original YouTube Music captures; only the surrounding background and
rounded framing were added.

- `dark/` contains the two screenshots on dark abstract backgrounds.
- `light/` contains the same screenshots on light abstract backgrounds.
- The two screenshots in this directory are copies of the dark set, preserving
  the paths used in the earlier Web Store update instructions.

- `backgrounds/orange-black-abstract-pawel-czerwinski.jpg` — [Orange and black fluid abstract painting](https://unsplash.com/photos/orange-and-black-fluid-abstract-painting-kNx8H8ScHDk)
- `backgrounds/purple-black-abstract-pawel-czerwinski.jpg` — [Purple and black abstract painting](https://unsplash.com/photos/purple-and-black-abstract-painting-72Mf6a3tpno)
- `backgrounds/beige-orange-abstract-pawel-czerwinski.jpg` — [Brown and white water splash](https://unsplash.com/photos/brown-and-white-water-splash-BFxnx0UHVGM)
- `backgrounds/pink-white-abstract-pawel-czerwinski.jpg` — [Pink and white abstract painting](https://unsplash.com/photos/pink-and-white-abstract-painting-fHaBDYb2dJg)
